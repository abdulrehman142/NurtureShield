NurtureShield - Result Artifacts
================================

All artifacts in this directory were generated from the
corrected NurtureShield research outputs.

Final production system:
- Model: HistGradientBoostingClassifier
- Feature count: 31
- Production threshold: 0.30

Important evaluation note:
- final_test_metrics.json stores held-out test results evaluated
  at threshold 0.24.
- The production threshold 0.30 was selected separately using
  validation threshold analysis.
- Therefore, the held-out confusion matrix must be reported as
  an evaluation-threshold-0.24 result, not as a threshold-0.30
  production confusion matrix.
